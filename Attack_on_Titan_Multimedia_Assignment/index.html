About Attack on Titan

Attack on Titan is a Japanese anime and manga series created by Hajime Isayama. The story takes place in a world where humanity lives behind enormous walls to protect themselves from giant creatures known as Titans.

The story follows Eren Yeager, Mikasa Ackerman, Armin Arlert, and other members of the Survey Corps as they discover the mysteries behind the Titans and the world around them.
